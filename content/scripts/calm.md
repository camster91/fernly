Welcome. For the next ten minutes, there is nothing you need to fix or figure out. Find a comfortable position, sitting or lying down, and let your hands rest wherever they fall.

[pause 5s]

Close your eyes if that feels okay, or let your gaze rest softly on one spot in front of you.

Take a slow breath in through your nose, and let it out through your mouth, as if you were gently fogging a window.

[pause 6s]

Once more. In... and out. Let your shoulders drop a little as you breathe out.

[pause 8s]

Now let your breathing find its own pace. You don't need to control it. Just notice it. Notice where you feel it most clearly, perhaps in the chest, the belly, or the cool air at the tip of the nose.

[pause 15s]

Your mind will wander. That's what minds do. When you notice it has drifted, simply come back to the breath. No need to scold yourself. Coming back is the practice.

[pause 20s]

Let's soften the body, starting with the face. Relax the forehead. Let the space between your eyebrows smooth out. Unclench your jaw and let your tongue rest in your mouth.

[pause 15s]

Soften your neck and shoulders. Let them be heavy. Let your arms feel loose, down to the fingertips.

[pause 15s]

Feel your chest rise and fall. Feel your belly soften. Let your back be held by whatever is supporting you, the chair, the bed, the floor.

[pause 20s]

Let your legs be heavy. Let your feet be still.

[pause 15s]

Now return to the breath. In, and out. If thoughts are busy, imagine them as clouds moving across a wide sky. You don't have to chase them or push them away. Just watch them pass.

[pause 30s]

Breathing in, you might silently say the word calm. Breathing out, the word easy. Calm, and easy. Use the words only if they help.

[pause 70s]

If you feel restless, that's fine too. Notice the restlessness with kindness, and keep breathing.

[pause 30s]

Rest here for a little while, with nothing to do and nowhere to be.

[pause 120s]

Now, slowly, begin to widen your attention. Notice the sounds in the room, and beyond the room. Notice the weight of your body.

[pause 10s]

Take a deeper breath in. Wiggle your fingers and toes. Stretch if you'd like to.

[pause 8s]

When you're ready, open your eyes. Thank yourself for taking this time. You can come back to this calm whenever you need it.
