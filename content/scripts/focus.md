Let's take five minutes to gather your attention. Sit upright but relaxed, with your feet on the floor and your hands resting on your lap.

[pause 5s]

Take a deep breath in through your nose, and a long breath out. Let your eyes close, or rest your gaze on the floor a little way ahead.

[pause 8s]

Feel the points where your body touches the chair. Feel your feet on the ground. You are here, in this moment, and that's enough to begin.

[pause 10s]

Now bring your attention to your breath at the nose. Notice the air coming in, a little cooler. Notice the air going out, a little warmer.

[pause 15s]

Follow one full breath, from the very beginning to the very end. Then another. Just one breath at a time.

[pause 20s]

At some point your mind will pull away, to a task, a message, a worry. Notice that gently, and bring your attention back to the next breath. Each time you return, you're practising focus, a little at a time.

[pause 15s]

Let's try counting. Breathe in, and as you breathe out, silently count one. In again, out, two. Keep going up to ten, then start over at one. If you lose count, that's okay. Just begin again at one.

[pause 20s]

Now let go of the counting. Rest your attention on the breath alone, steady and simple.

[pause 30s]

Think for a moment about what you'd like to do next. Don't plan it all out. Just choose one thing, and picture yourself giving it your full attention.

[pause 20s]

Take a deeper breath in. Feel the energy gather. Let it out with a slow sigh.

[pause 8s]

Gently open your eyes, and carry this clear attention into whatever comes next.
