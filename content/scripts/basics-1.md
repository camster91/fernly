Welcome to the basics. This is the first of two short sessions to help you start a meditation practice. There's nothing to get right here.

[pause 5s]

Meditation is simply paying attention, on purpose, with kindness. In this session, we'll use the breath as the place to rest our attention.

[pause 8s]

First, find a position. Sit on a chair with your feet flat on the floor, or sit on a cushion, or lie down. Keep your back comfortably upright, and let your hands rest on your lap.

[pause 10s]

You can close your eyes, or lower your gaze to a spot on the floor.

[pause 8s]

Take three slow breaths. In through the nose, out through the mouth. Let your body settle with each one.

[pause 15s]

Now let your breath return to normal. You don't need to change it. Just notice it.

[pause 10s]

Where do you feel your breath most clearly? In the nostrils, the chest, or the belly? Choose one spot, and rest your attention there.

[pause 5s]

Feel one breath arrive, and feel it leave. There's a small pause between them. See if you can notice that.

[pause 10s]

You may find your mind has wandered already. That's completely normal, and it happens to everyone. When you notice, gently come back to the breath. That moment of noticing is the heart of meditation.

[pause 15s]

Keep resting your attention on the breath. Each time you drift, return without judgement.

[pause 20s]

If it helps, you can silently say in as you breathe in, and out as you breathe out.

[pause 20s]

Now, let the labels go. Just rest with the breath for a little longer.

[pause 10s]

Let's finish by noticing how you feel. Maybe calmer, maybe the same. Either is fine. You've just practised something worthwhile.

[pause 8s]

Take a deeper breath, move your fingers and toes, and open your eyes when you're ready. Next time, we'll talk about the wandering mind.
