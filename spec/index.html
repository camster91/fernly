01.5 · BRAND MARK EXPLORATION

Four fern marks. Pick one.

Each is a different read on "fern" — the brand's visual hook. All four work at 22px (favicon), 64px (splash), 1024px (App Store), and as a single-color knockout. Real SVG, no raster placeholders.

A · Sprig — three rising fronds
The most literal read of a fern. Three fronds at different heights, all rising from a single base. Reads as "growing" instantly. Risk: verges on plant-shop / wellness cliché.
1024px · full
64px · favicon
knockout · on dark
22px · micro
Literal 3-color Strong at all sizes Risk: generic plant icon
B · Unfurl — single coiled frond
One fern frond mid-unfurl — the spiral fiddlehead shape. Most iconic fern visual. Says "becoming" / "in-progress" / "alive" in a way a sprig can't. Risk: spiral overload (Headspace-adjacent).
1024px · full
64px · favicon
knockout · on dark
22px · micro
Symbolic 2-color Distinctive spiral Risk: ornamentation at 22px
C · Soft dot — circular silence
No fern at all. Just a soft pastel disc with a single darker mark inside — a quiet pulse, a held breath. Anti-illustration. Stands out in a sea of plant-themed wellness apps. Risk: not obviously "fern."
1024px · full
64px · favicon
knockout · on dark
22px · micro
Abstract 2-color + disc Most legible at small sizes Risk: no fern = no name connection
D · Wordmark — just the F
Most apps in this space use illustrations. A custom wordmark with a single stylized "f" as the icon is a deliberate move against the trend. Lower design cost, higher brand-equity risk. Risk: hard to be distinctive with one letter.

DIRECTION LOCKED
Fern frond illustration (Option 2)
Of three AI-generated concept renders, the fern-frond illustration was the only one that read instantly as "fern" — the paired leaflets along the central stem are the unmistakable botanical signature. It needs a real designer to simplify it for icon use: strip the surrounding leaves, keep the central frond with 5–7 leaflet pairs, snap to a 24px grid, ship as vector at 1024/512/180/120/64/22px. DESIGNER BRIEF · paste into Fiverr/99designs
Project: App icon + wordmark for Fernly, a wellness companion iOS/Android app.
Direction: Reference concept at fernly-spec/screens/_ai_render_frond.jpeg — single fern frond with paired leaflets.
Required deliverables:
  1. App icon: 1024×1024 master, simplified from concept (single frond, 5–7 leaflet pairs, no surrounding leaves)
  2. Wordmark: custom-drawn fernly lowercase, geometric sans, weight-matched to icon
  3. Lockup: icon + wordmark horizontal
  4. Knockout versions: white-on-dark and dark-on-light
  5. Source files: Figma + SVG (vector-clean, no rasters)
  6. PNG exports: 1024, 512, 180, 120, 64, 48, 32, 22 (favicon)
Palette: Lavender #9B7DD4 primary · Mint #8FDDB4 accent · Ink #2A2438 on light
Don't: generic plant icon · Headspace-style single orange dot · serif typography · hand-drawn ornamentation · anything resembling Calm's leaf or Earkick's chat bubble
Budget: $150–300 · turnaround 5–7 days · unlimited revisions until readable at 22px

END · NOTES FOR REVIEW

What to push back on.

Things I'd flag if you asked me to be honest about the mocks:

🌿 Brand mark is placeholder.
Current mark uses CSS gradients as a stand-in fern frond. Replace with real SVG/illustrated mark before launch — ship a designed mark in week 7.
🤖 The mascot might feel young.
Moon/fox/turtle is Gen Z-coded. If 25–35 yr old ICP, soften to abstract marks or text-only.
💰 Paywall trigger feels early.
3 chats/day hits by Tuesday afternoon for heavy users. Consider 5/day or week-based quota.
🆘 Crisis copy is the most important pixel.
Have a real clinician or crisis counselor write the exact wording on screen 25 before launch.
📊 Weekly insights need real grounding.
"Sundays are harder" needs a minimum sample size and honest confidence level. Don't ship a fluke.
🌍 "International" deserves more than a link.
Crisis resources should localize by device locale. 988 only works in US.
