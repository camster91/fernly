# Code of Conduct — Fernly

This Code of Conduct applies to all Fernly community spaces — GitHub Issues, PRs, commit messages, code reviews, and any future Discord/Slack. It also applies to anyone representing Fernly publicly (blog posts, conference talks).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Fernly a welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We especially pledge to be a good-faith participant in conversations about mental health, crisis intervention, and clinical UX. Many of our users (and some of our contributors) have first-hand experience with the situations Fernly's crisis detector is designed to catch. **The way we talk to each other in this repo is part of what we ship.**

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Welcoming** new contributors, regardless of skill level
- **Patient** with questions, even when answering the same thing the 5th time
- **Curious** about decisions, especially safety-related ones
- **Honest** about uncertainty — say "I'm not sure" out loud
- **Careful** with crisis-flagged content: don't post the word, mock it, or generate it without a clinical-UX rationale
- **Kind** in disagreement — critique the work, not the person
- **Concrete** in code review — "this could fail when X" beats "this is wrong"

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information ("doxxing")
- Mocking, dismissing, or generating crisis-flagged content ("let's see what 'kms' does")
- Deliberately triggering CrisisModal in a demo/screenshot without disclosing the test
- Any of the above in commit messages, PR descriptions, or issue comments

## Our responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all Fernly community spaces and also applies when a contributor is representing Fernly in public spaces. Examples of representing Fernly include using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the team at `conduct@fernly.app`. **For security issues, see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead.**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.
